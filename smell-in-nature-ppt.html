Smell feels immersive,
but do you know how?

Every scent is really tiny invisible molecules floating through the air. Your nose catches them — and your brain does the rest. Tap each step:

1
Scent molecules drift into your nose
Flowers, food, even rain release molecules that travel through the air until they reach your nostrils.
2
Smell receptors catch them
Deep inside your nose, tiny receptors are shaped to match different scent molecules — like little locks and keys.
3
A signal zooms to your brain
Your olfactory nerve sends the message straight to the brain's emotion and memory centers — faster than any other sense!
4
You react — before you even think
That's why a smell can bring back a memory or a feeling almost instantly, before you've even named it.
